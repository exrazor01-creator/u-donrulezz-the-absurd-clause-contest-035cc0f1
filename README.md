# The Absurd Clause Contest

**Entry:** "Section 14.3: by accepting these terms, you grant us a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to your dreams, including the recurring one about the exam you never studied for."

**Characters:** 172 (gate: 1–200, one sentence)

## Why it fits the brief

1. **On subject:** an absurd clause that could hide deep inside a terms-of-service agreement.
2. **Funny on its own:** the joke lands on one reading, with no setup — the licence quietly covers "your dreams".
3. **Real boilerplate until the absurdity hits:** "Section 14.3", "by accepting these terms", "non-exclusive, royalty-free licence" — the register does the work.
4. **Original and specific:** the concrete absurd image is the recurring exam dream you never studied for, a dream nearly everyone has had, now owned under licence.
5. **Tight:** one clean sentence, no wasted words, no explaining of its own joke.
